Frequently Asked

Before you hit send

A few things I get asked often — might save us both a round-trip email.

How do you charge?

Monthly retainers for ongoing growth work (SEO, paid, content). Fixed-fee for audits and full-stack builds. I'll always share a clear scope before you commit.

What's the typical engagement length?

SEO and content take 3–6 months to compound, so I prefer 6+ month engagements there. Paid social can start showing wins in weeks. Audits and builds are one-off.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes — I white-label technical SEO audits and dev work for a handful of trusted agencies. Get in touch with your agency name and I'll send rates.

What time zone do you work in?

PKT (UTC+5). I have 3–4 hours of overlap with most European clients and Australian East Coast, and async-first overlap with US clients.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. Happy to sign yours, or I can send a mutual NDA before our first call if your project is sensitive.

Can you fix a Google penalty?

In most cases yes — manual actions, algorithmic hits, and indexation problems. Send me the affected domain and I'll do a free first-look diagnostic.
