A/B Testing 101 · By Wajeeh

What this playground simplifies

Real A/B testing is messier than this UI suggests — but the principles you just felt are how I actually run tests for clients.

01 · CONFIDENCE

Statistical Significance

You need at least 100 conversions per variant and 95% confidence before declaring a winner. Anything less is gut-feeling dressed up in math.

02 · ISOLATION

One Variable at a Time

Change the headline and the CTA color together, and you'll never know which one moved the needle. Real tests change one thing.

03 · BIAS

Selection Bias is Real

If your "winner" only beat the control during a flash sale, you tested the offer — not the copy. Always check sample composition.

04 · TIME

Run Tests Through Full Cycles

Buyer behavior on Monday differs from Saturday. Tests should run at least one full week — ideally two — to catch cyclical patterns.

05 · IMPACT

Effect Sizes Matter

A 2% lift on a page that gets 100 visitors/month is noise. Prioritize tests on pages with traffic and revenue weight.

06 · GLOBAL

Local Wins ≠ Global Wins

The variant that wins on the homepage may lose on a landing page with different intent. Test in context, deploy in context.
